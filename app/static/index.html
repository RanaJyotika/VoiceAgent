How it works

  1. Create a campaign. Pick what the calls are for: confirming orders, confirming bookings, or inviting customers to a campaign.
  2. Upload its contact sheet. Download the template for that kind of call, fill it in, and upload it.
  3. Start calling. The agent rings everyone on the list and writes down what each person said.
